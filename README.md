# MT — Master Trade

`MT.mq5` is a MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor with a market panel, independent timeframe readings, informational trade plans, an observed-market diary and optional broker auto trading. **Auto Trading defaults to false**; when enabled, it can send real orders. Copy `MT.mq5` **and all seven `MT_*.mqh` files** into the same `MQL5/Experts` folder, compile in MetaEditor, and attach it to a supported chart. Native compilation, MT5/broker integration and out-of-sample target performance are not yet verified.

## Panel

The default appearance remains Consolas 10 pt on `C'48,48,48'`, with the title in white at 11 pt. The EA measures the displayed text using MT5 font metrics and sizes the box to its current content, including the detail/notice line; a conservative DPI-based estimate is used if text measurement fails. Supported charts reserve three header rows followed by the three timeframe lanes. Row 1 contains the header; row 2 is blank when open and shows market details when closed or abnormal; row 3 remains reserved. Width and height are measured to cover all displayed content. Long lines are drawn as multiple short labels to avoid text truncation.

- M5, M15 and M30: the header shows the broker's symbol, its best-effort asset class and market state. While open it shows server time in the header and the three timeframe lanes below the three-row header area; row 2 is blank, with no clock-estimation guidance. When closed it shows `Open at` and the next regular session, plus an Automate-style `MARKET CLOSED - next open ... server (in ...)` detail.
- Other timeframes: the header identifies the symbol and timeframe and the two-line panel shows SimpleEA's `Incompatible chart. Switch to M5/M15/M30.` notice.
- The weekly broker trade-session table is refreshed every five minutes. Sessions may cross midnight. If it is unavailable, a fresh quote can indicate an open market; without a fresh quote the state is unknown and `Open at --` is shown rather than an invented opening time. Disconnection, broker-disabled symbols, and stale quotes are distinguished from a scheduled close.

The next opening is a **regular weekly schedule**, not a guarantee: holidays, broker halts, and changed schedules can invalidate it. `00:00–00:00` is treated as a 24-hour session. Classification uses broker metadata and name heuristics and can be wrong; it does not affect trading.

## Phase 2 readings

Each lane is calculated independently for the chart symbol on the **closed bars** of M5, M15 or M30:

- **GT**: Automate's 30-bar regression slope normalized by the median absolute change of 20 bars; UP/DOWN outside ±0.20, otherwise FLAT.
- **CT**: SimpleEA's adaptive Precision Trend filter sign. Its adaptive short cycle is 14–36 (24 when there are too few crossings); the long period is approximately 6.25 times the short, clamped to 90–225. CT is FLAT at exactly zero.
- **ST**: matching directions give UP or DOWN, two FLATs give FLAT, opposed directions give CONFLICT, and one FLAT with a direction gives MIXED. An unavailable component gives WAIT.
- **RG**: SimpleEA's Hurst estimator on that lane's own data, never a borrowed timeframe. It uses 1,536/512/256 bars for M5/M15/M30, plus the preceding close. Above 0.55 is STEADY, below 0.45 CHOPPY, otherwise MIXED. Invalid data or insufficient history gives WAIT, not a substituted 0.5.
- **SC**: SimpleEA's three votes: CT direction, its ATR-deadbanded rate of change, and 20-bar net movement with confirmed swing checks and an ATR deadband. It ranges from -3/3 to +3/3. GT, ST, RG and PT do not change the score.

**PT** estimates the remaining life of the selected label: M5 CT, M15 ST and M30 GT. **%L** beside SC independently estimates the share of the exact signed score's estimated life remaining. Each uses the median remaining length of at least 20 fully observed past runs of the same label that reached the current age, including zero remaining. A run beginning before the replay window is not counted. Historical score and adaptive periods are reconstructed causally, without training or waiting for new trades. Estimates can be inaccurate or biased by changes in market conditions or the available historical window.

The countdown basis is held when a state starts or the EA attaches mid-state. A positive life fraction that rounds below one percent is shown as `<1%L`, never as an expired zero. Each subsequent closed bar reduces the remaining count. PT shows `~0b` at its estimate and `0b+` afterward; score life shows `~0%L` and then `past est.`. Each first label change resets the respective countdown, including `+3` to `+2`. Terminal global variables preserve a valid basis across restarts where possible; MT verifies the observed label and age across the intervening bars before restoring it. `--`/`--%L` means that duration is unavailable, not zero. **Neither PT nor %L is a trade-success probability or a guarantee of a change.**

MT requests closed history directly from the terminal, retries synchronization and progressively increases the request when estimates lack samples, up to the **terminal's configured maximum bars per timeframe** (excluding the forming bar). The broker's history, synchronization, terminal settings, invalid data and available memory can limit what is available. If a deeper `CopyRates` request fails, the EA retries the deeper request and can retain independently supported readings from a smaller available range; it never substitutes a regime value. With short history, already supported GT or RG may display while CT/SC still show WAIT. The filter uses a 700-bar warm-up and the finite historical replay can differ slightly from SimpleEA's live rolling pull because recursive filter initialization is not identical. Large history requests and calculations can take time, memory and CPU and may briefly block MT5; data are refreshed on new bars and incomplete history is retried. Phase 2 itself does not place orders; Phase 3 adds the inputs and informational planning described below.

## Server clock

MT5 has one trade-server time for the account, not a separate clock per symbol. In `OnTick`, `TimeCurrent()` is the server-stamped time of the chart symbol's handled quote. Outside `OnTick`, it is the most recent quote time for **any** Market Watch symbol. The panel anchors to new broker timestamps and advances between them using a monotonic counter. This avoids the previous reliance on `TimeTradeServer()`, which MetaTrader calculates from the PC's clock settings. After startup and before a new quote is observed, `TimeTradeServer()` is the only running estimate available. In Strategy Tester, simulated `TimeCurrent()` is used instead of real elapsed time.

A timestamp in a quote has second precision and may be delayed in transit; extrapolation between quotes and over long closures can drift, and a server clock or timezone change can occur without a new quote. **Perfect synchronization cannot be guaranteed by MT5's APIs.** The clock is estimated between quotes. Compare it with Market Watch's server time on a live connection, and check the PC clock if the panel starts before any fresh quote.

## Phase 3 — informational plans and diary

The panel preserves the three header rows and Phase 2 lanes, then shows three plan lines, the symbol's bid/ask and three diary summaries. `Show diary details` adds one detail line per timeframe. Plan lines contain BUY/SELL/NONE, market or limit entry, lot, SL and TP prices and estimated SL/TP amounts in account currency. Plan amounts are gross `OrderCalcProfit` estimates; a price outcome is not an executed trade. The box still resizes for all rows and label segments.

Inputs: `Trend to follow M5/M15/M30` are four-choice inputs (Auto/CT/GT/ST), default Auto; `Allow against trend M5/M15/M30` default true. `Bypass risk`, `Minimum lot use`, and `Show diary details` default false. Normal sizing targets 0.5% of equity per plan, using the entry/SL loss estimated by `OrderCalcProfit`; broker min/max/step apply and below-minimum lots become the broker minimum. Minimum lot use forces that minimum. Bypass uses SimpleEA's balance ladder (0.01 lot per 1,000 account-currency balance units, 1.5 times at absolute score 3). It is not normalized across currencies. These Phase 3 inputs do not enable trading; Phase 4 adds the separate Auto Trading input.

A score of ±3 uses a post-close Ask/Bid reference for market entry; ±2 uses a confirmed swing limit or a fallback derived from the median recent directional bar move. SL uses a confirmed opposite swing with spread/tick protection, or a recent-move fallback. For TP, MT reconstructs completed same-signed-score historical bar analogues, measures favorable movement before the stop over 4/6/8 bars for M5/M15/M30, and uses the median favorable movement relative to stop risk when at least 20 analogues exist. On Bid-based charts, a limit touch is assessed using bar-level spread proxies; stop wins ambiguous bars, and the entry bar contributes no favorable extreme. On Last-based charts, or when the chart price type is unavailable, Bid-specific replay is disabled; recent-move fallback and chart-bar structure are used as a clearly labelled Last/unknown price proxy. With insufficient analogues or a zero median it also uses a recent-move fallback (one tick if no positive directional bar movement is available). TP is capped by an opposing pivot where compatible with broker-distance constraints. These are initial design choices, not optimized or validated claims. Historical replay is not the diary, and does not use future bars at each historical decision. Entry and plan geometry can be unavailable if required quotes, history or contract data are unavailable; BUY/SELL remains visible.

Each timeframe captures its own first valid quote observed by the EA on a tick after its latest bar closes. Closed-bar structure plus that frozen Bid/Ask (including its spread) produces a snapshot of entry, SL, TP, lot and estimated amounts; panel refreshes and other timeframes' bar closes do not reprice it. This is not a pure OHLC-only plan: market BUY references the captured Ask and SELL the captured Bid, and limit geometry also uses the quote and spread. If attached/restarted mid-bar, after a gap, or if MT5 coalesces ticks, the first quote observed by the EA may not be the broker's actual first quote after the close. A snapshot is rebuilt after reinitialization, not persisted. If history arrives late, the same captured quote is used; if no valid post-close tick is observed, no priced plan is invented. Once priced, a plan with unavailable sizing/amounts stays unavailable until the next lane bar or reinitialization. If retried historical readings change the signal or market/limit type within a bar, an incompatible frozen plan is held rather than silently repriced.

The diary records observed broker quote touches, not fills or realized profit. It counts one idea when a lane enters a new directional signal, rather than on every tick; the same direction is not re-counted until it changes or becomes NONE. If a plan or tick snapshot is unavailable, it records `AWAIT DATA` and can start that same idea later using the then-current frozen plan and real observed quotes while its direction remains current. The frozen market entry is a reference; if observation starts later, its actual starting quote is recorded separately. A market observation waits if its start quote has already passed an exit; the start tick is checked for exits and limit touches. Original signal context and time and later plan context and time are preserved separately; no earlier touch is inferred. Once a plan starts, entry, SL and TP are frozen. Awaiting/pending/open/expired/unknown are not wins or losses. Awaiting and untouched pending observations expire if their lane changes direction or becomes NONE; open observations continue to their original TP/SL. Signals count on their broker-server signal day, wins/losses on their resolution day, and all-time begins with the local diary. The live file is in MT5 Common Files keyed by account, server and symbol; tester files are isolated. It uses a single-writer lock and versioned snapshot. Existing version-3 and version-4 diary records migrate to version 5; version-3 bar-price provenance remains unknown. A second instance cannot record to the same diary. `CopyTicksRange` requests intermediate bid/ask ticks; failure, disconnection, or a mismatch between the tick-history endpoint and quote snapshot makes affected active outcomes unknown. Open/pending observations become unknown on restart because continuous coverage is not certified. MT5 may supply incomplete tick history even without an identifiable error, so the diary is not a broker execution ledger. Hiding details does not stop recording. Changing an MT5 input reinitializes the EA, so already active observations become unknown rather than claiming continuous coverage.

Run `python3 tests/test_phase3_kernels.py`, `python3 tests/test_plan_kernels.py`, `python3 tests/test_plan_runtime.py`, `python3 tests/test_diary_kernels.py` and `python3 tests/test_diary_runtime.py` in addition to the earlier suites. These test actual source through C++ stubs, not MetaEditor or a broker. Test on a demo terminal: compile all files; inspect plan prices, tick size, stops and account-currency amounts; check three timeframe inputs and countertrend switches; compare recorded touch order with tick history; test restart, connection loss, two charts, file corruption and broker-day rollover. Target quality needs independent out-of-sample tick data with spread, non-fill and cost assumptions before relying on it.

## Phase 4 — broker auto trading

`Auto Trading` defaults to false. When true, and MT5/account permissions permit, MT may submit the existing plan: score ±3 uses a market order and ±2 a limit order, with the plan lot, SL and one TP. Following SimpleEA's account handling, **new entries require a retail hedging account**; netting/exchange accounts are held. There can be one active order/position per timeframe. Once broker records prove it has ended, the same still-valid signal can enter again. Before a new order, MT checks frozen entry/SL/TP against the live quote and broker stop distance and runs `OrderCheck`; it holds an invalid plan rather than chasing or moving its levels. Market requests use the live Ask/Bid, so the fill and risk can differ from the plan reference and estimates. On a new own-timeframe plan, MT can request a pending entry/SL/TP modification after live-quote, spread, lot/risk and broker checks; broker-confirmed levels remain authoritative. Open positions and their broker-side exits are not modified by this rule. Requests are throttled to at most one attempt per lane every five seconds. New orders require a symbol quote no more than 10 seconds old by the broker-anchored clock. MT also requires broker-reported support for the requested order type and both SL and TP, plus a GTC stop-lifetime mode that preserves protective levels across days; symbols without that information are held.

MT requests cancellation of an owned pending limit when the lane signal changes direction, becomes NONE or WAIT, the plan becomes unavailable/unsuitable, the target distance does not clear the current spread, or the order lacks SL/TP. A same-direction upgrade to a market signal cancels the limit rather than changing it into a market order. A pending order also expires after three actual completed bars of its own timeframe after broker-confirmed placement; modifying prices does not restart its age. After either event the order must be broker-reconciled. After expiry, a repeat of the same signal waits for another close of that timeframe; after a market upgrade, any replacement waits for another own-timeframe close. Unknown birth/history is handled conservatively by cancellation, not by resetting the age. Disabling Auto Trading requests cancellation of MT's pending limits and prevents new entries; it does not close an open position. Once a cancellation is requested it stays pending until confirmed by the broker, even if the signal changes again. A partial fill's unfilled remainder is requested for cancellation; the filled position is left open with its broker-side SL/TP. An owned pending order observed without SL or TP is requested for cancellation; an open position missing either is shown as `SL/TP missing: REVIEW` and is not automatically closed or modified. If MT5 or the broker does not permit a cancellation, MT cannot claim it succeeded and will retry while the EA is running. Removing the EA or using an unsupported chart stops its management; it does not itself cancel pending orders or close positions.

Three auto-trade rows appear above the diary. They show broker-confirmed positions and limits, not plan or price-touch simulations: direction, OPEN/LIMIT, entry, lot, SL/TP and their estimated gross entry-to-level amounts in USD. A limit's price distance is Ask minus entry for BUY or entry minus Bid for SELL; a negative distance means the level has been passed. On USD accounts, `OrderCalcProfit` is already in USD; otherwise MT searches selected, fresh direct/inverse Forex quotes for conversion and shows `-- USD` if none are available. These estimates do not include fees. The Phase 3 diary remains an observed-quote diary, not a broker execution ledger.

Auto trading uses per-account/server/symbol shared-file coordination and durable pre-send and pending-modification intents, with a separate tester sandbox. The version-2 journal migrates version-1 records; old pending orders without a verifiable birth bar are requested for cancellation, while confirmed open positions are retained. A modification keeps the broker order volume unchanged; if that volume exceeds the new plan lot (or violates the forced minimum), it is cancelled rather than silently increasing the planned risk. A new modification is not considered effective until the broker shows matching levels; an unconfirmed modification is conservatively cancelled and reconciled. Requests are prechecked with `OrderCheck`; a successful send alone is not a confirmed fill. MT checks broker order/position identities and history before reopening a slot. An ambiguous result, unknown ownership, multiple same-lane objects, unavailable history, a corrupt journal or failed save holds the affected trading instead of blindly repeating. Only the instance holding the lock manages that account/symbol; other instances show the coordinator as unavailable until reinitialized. The lock coordinates terminals sharing the same Common Files on the same machine; it cannot coordinate separate machines. Do not run this EA for the same account and symbol on separate machines without external coordination. The account-mode and protective-level checks apply to new entries; already owned pending orders are still considered for cancellation when disabled.

Source-level C++ broker/file stubs exercise placement, netting hold, disabled and cancellation behavior, re-entry, partial-fill/cancel races, restarts, uncertain outcomes, USD conversion and corrupt storage. Run `python3 tests/test_auto_policy.py` and `python3 tests/test_auto_runtime.py` alongside the earlier suites. They are **not** MetaEditor or MT5 tests. Native compilation, Strategy Tester/demo tests, modification/cancellation and fill races, bar-history recovery, broker return codes, currency conversion feeds and real locking/persistence have not been verified; use those checks before enabling live auto trading.
