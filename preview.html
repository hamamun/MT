Fixed height
Four rows of InpFontSize + 11 = 21 px, so the box is 84 px tall at 10 pt. Rows 2–4 stay empty until later phases fill them.
Clock
The mockup uses your local clock. The EA uses TimeTradeServer() and a one-second timer, so it ticks even when no ticks arrive.
At 10 pt on a 96 DPI display the monospaced advance is 8 px; the 11 pt title is 9 px. The box is the wider state (520 px) plus one character of padding on each side (16 px) = 536 px. The open state leaves the extra space empty on the right; the closed state fills it.
