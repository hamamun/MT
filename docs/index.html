01 / THE BIG PICTUREOVERVIEW

One workspace.
Two perspectives.

MT_CRF combines MT’s three independent timeframe lanes with an M15 confirmed reversal forecast. Choose how they work together, then use the panel to follow readings, plans and broker state.

The EA can observe without trading. When enabled, automated execution is subject to its inputs, history, market conditions, broker permissions and reconciliation checks. MT and CRF are distinct strategies; a forecast is not a promise of an order.

02 / FIRST THINGS FIRSTINSTALLATION

From files to first look.

You need MetaTrader 5, MetaEditor and the source files from this repository. Keep the files together so the EA can find its includes.

01

Get the files

Download the repository from GitHub or clone it. Copy MT_CRF.mq5 and all MT_*.mqh files into the same MQL5/Experts folder. In MT5, use File → Open Data Folder to locate it.

02

Compile in MetaEditor

Open MT_CRF.mq5 in MetaEditor and compile. Resolve any errors shown in the editor before attaching the EA. MT.mq5 is the separate original EA.

03

Attach & observe

Refresh the Navigator if needed, then attach MT_CRF to the desired symbol on an M5, M15 or M30 chart. Allow history to synchronize and review the mode, inputs and panel. Other chart timeframes show an incompatible-chart notice.

04

Choose when to automate

Auto trading starts off. To test orders, use a demo account, enable the master input and the relevant MT5/EA permissions, and check the timer, account type and broker conditions. Input changes reinitialize the EA.

03 / CHOOSE YOUR VIEWMODES

Set the working mode.

The Mode input controls which strategy runs. It does not turn the master auto-trading switch on.

DEFAULT

MT + CRF

All three MT lanes run. CRF scans M15 in the background and can take over the existing M15 slot when eligible. M5 and M30 continue their normal rules.

02

MT only

Use native MT readings, plans and execution; CRF scanning and CRF orders are off.

03

CRF only

Use the M15-shaped CRF view and execution. New native MT orders are disabled; existing exposure still requires reconciliation.

↳

One M15 slot, not two. In combined mode, CRF may cancel a native M15 pending order or, under its separate profitable-takeover rules, request an existing native M15 position close. It waits for broker confirmation and the next M15 close before an eligible entry. M5/M30 are not closed by this takeover.

04 / KNOW WHAT YOU'RE SEEINGTHE PANEL

Read the room,
then the trade.

The chart panel separates market information, a proposed plan and actual broker execution. Values can be unavailable while data is loading or when broker information is missing.

Market readings

Each lane uses its own closed-bar data. During CRF takeover, its M15 row reflects the CRF view instead.

GT
Global Trend: the broader recent trend for that timeframe.
CT
Current Trend: an adaptive, more responsive trend reading.
ST
Status: GT and CT combined. Opposite directions are CONFLICT; direction plus flat is MIXED; unavailable data is WAIT.
RG
Regime: STEADY, MIXED or CHOPPY from that lane’s history; WAIT means insufficient or invalid data.
PT
Approximate remaining life of the selected trend label, in bars: CT on M5, ST on M15 and GT on M30. 0b+ means it has passed the estimate.
SC / %L
Score from −3 to +3 and an independent estimate of the exact score’s remaining life. Neither %L nor PT is a trade-success probability.
SG
The lane’s plan signal: BUY, SELL, NONE or WAIT.
THE DISTINCTION

A plan is not
an order.

Trading Plans shows the calculated entry, lot, SL and TP when available. These levels are a proposal, not proof of a fill.

Auto Execution shows broker order/position state or the reason it is blocked. OPEN is a position; LIMIT is a pending order. A request remains subject to broker confirmation.

READINGS → PLAN → EXECUTION

Other panel details

Header & settings. The header shows symbol, market state, time and Today / All trade counts. Settings show the selected input states. A closed-market view collapses to the header and next scheduled opening when available.

Execution values. Entry, lot, SL, TP and floating P/L reflect broker state when available. P/L is POSITION_PROFIT, excluding swap and commission. TP distance uses the exit-side quote: Bid for BUY, Ask for SELL.

CRF details. With CRF Details on, the separate details window includes status, pattern, candidate, data-through time, entry/trigger, SL, target, estimated move and duration, quality, follow-through, sample count, expiry, reason and last result.

Chart visibility. On supported charts the EA requests that MT5 hide chart trade levels and history markers while attached, restoring prior visibility when removed. This does not alter the trades.

THE PLAN / FIELD NOTESPLAN & EXECUTION

The numbers in a trade row

Signal & style
BUY or SELL is direction. MARKET describes an immediate-order plan; LIMIT describes a pending-entry plan. NONE means no direction; WAIT means required data is unavailable.
Volume: OK / LOW / --
Native MT’s latest closed-bar tick-volume check. LOW can change a score-3 plan from market to limit; -- means volume is unavailable, not zero.
Entry & lot
Entry is the planned or broker price, depending on the section. Lot is the requested or broker volume. CRF creates MARKET plans only. Its reference entry is the confirmation close; requests use BUY Ask or SELL Bid and the broker fill can differ. The lot may show -- until checks succeed, then stays frozen for that plan; execution can still be blocked.
SL & TP
The stop-loss and single take-profit price. The plan can also show estimated account-currency amounts; these are estimates, not realized results. A planned native SL is not broker protection when SL Placement is off.
TP distance & quote
Distance is in price units from BUY Bid or SELL Ask to TP; a negative value means the target was passed. The execution row can also show the entry-side quote: Ask for BUY, Bid for SELL.
Auto state & P/L
OPEN is a broker position; LIMIT is a pending order, not a fill. Floating P/L belongs to an open position. A CRF value marked last known is stale or cached; -- means unavailable. Blocked and REVIEW states explain why action cannot proceed.
THE FORECAST / FIELD NOTESCRF DETAILS

What the CRF details mean

Turn on CRF Details to open these fields in the separate details window. When there is no active forecast, last-value labels can retain the previous forecast rather than describe a new trade.

Direction & status
BUY/SELL indicates a confirmed direction; a question mark denotes a pending candidate. Status describes what CRF is doing or waiting for.
Pattern & candidate
The detected candle-pattern name and any candidate awaiting confirmation. A candidate is not yet a confirmed forecast.
Data through
The latest M15 bar processed by the forecast, useful when checking history and synchronization.
Entry / trigger, SL & TP
Forecast reference levels: entry/trigger, stop loss and estimated target. A CRF market request uses the live executable quote, so its actual entry may differ.
Expected move & duration
Historical analog-based move and typical duration estimates. The typical duration is not the hard deadline.
Estimate quality
The reference forecast’s quality classification; it is separate from the adjustable auto-entry sample and follow-through gates.
Follow-through / samples
The historical follow-through percentage and the number of comparable historical samples used in the estimate.
Forecast expires in
The remaining M15 bars to the upper duration limit, the forecast’s hard expiry. It is not a promise of a position exit price.
No-signal reason & last result
Why a usable signal or estimate is unavailable, plus the latest forecast outcome or update. Forecast outcomes are distinct from broker-confirmed trade results.

02

Trading & risk

Control permission, protection and position size.

Auto trading

InpAutoTrading
FALSE

Master switch for new MT and CRF orders and software-close requests. Off blocks those requests and requests cancellation of owned native pending orders; open positions remain subject to their existing broker SL/TP. It does not close open positions simply because it is switched off.

Stop Loss Placement

InpPlaceStopLoss
FALSE

Controls broker SL placement for new native MT orders. Off still shows a planned SL for sizing and validation, but it is not a protective broker stop. CRF orders always require broker SL and TP regardless of this setting. Existing positions are not modified by changing it.

Bypass Risk

InpBypassRisk
FALSE

Uses a balance-based lot ladder instead of normal stop-distance risk sizing. Native MT applies its score-3 multiplier; CRF uses its own balance ladder without the MT-score multiplier. This shared switch does not bypass CRF reward/risk filters; CRF risk bypass is separate.

Minimum Lot use

InpMinimumLot
FALSE

When true, request the broker’s minimum lot, subject to broker and execution checks. Otherwise normal sizing targets 0.5% of equity using the planned stop; valid calculated sizes below the broker minimum are rounded up to that minimum. Broker min/max/step still apply.

06 / HOW IT WORKSEXECUTION & EXITS

From signal to broker.

The EA checks permissions and broker state before it sends an order. A broker-confirmed state, not a chart touch or a successful send alone, determines what actually happened.

01SignalClosed-bar reading or confirmed CRF forecast
02ChecksInputs, history, market, quote and broker
03RequestPre-check and durable intent before send
04ReconcileWait for broker orders, positions and deals

Native MT orders

A qualifying ±3 score uses a market-style plan unless volume is weak; ±2 uses a limit. Pending limits are managed, can be cancelled when conditions change and expire after three completed own-timeframe bars. A confirmed cancellation or close is followed by a wait for the next close on that timeframe before replacement.

CRF orders

CRF uses M15 closed-bar confirmation, historical filters and market requests with one mandatory broker SL and TP. Live quote, spread, broker support, account permissions and reward/risk checks apply. Entries require a retail hedging account and a free, reconciled M15 slot.

CRF-ONLY EXIT / DEFAULT ON

How CRF trailstop works

↘
01 / ACTIVATE

At the earlier of two completed M15 candles (the fill candle counts as the first) or the exit quote reaching 50% of actual entry-to-TP distance. BUY uses Bid; SELL uses Ask. The activation candle becomes the reference.

02 / LOOK FOR TWO

For BUY, each of the next two completed candles must open below the previous close and close below its own open. For SELL, both comparisons are reversed. Equality does not qualify; later pairs may qualify.

03 / CHECK PROFIT

At a qualifying pattern, broker floating POSITION_PROFIT must be strictly positive. Zero or loss skips that pattern. A close request is checked again for profit and safety, sent for the full position and reconciled with the broker.

This does not modify SL/TP. The floating value excludes commission and swap; execution and net result may differ. If a final safety or profit check fails, that pattern is consumed. After restart, old patterns are not replayed; an offline intrabar crossing cannot be reconstructed exactly.
↳

Independent exits remain in place. Broker SL/TP, forecast expiry and newer confirmed-signal exits are separate from trailstop and remain active under their existing rules, even when trailstop is off or the position is losing. The master switch blocks software close requests; broker-side SL/TP remain in place.

07 / THE RECORDDIARY & DATA

A record of what happened.

Broker-trade diary

The diary records broker-confirmed auto-order and deal information by account, server, symbol and lane. Today uses the broker server day; All starts with the local ledger. S counts accepted orders; W, L and BE count fully reconciled closed positions by net deal result. The compact header omits BE; the details view includes it.

Partial, unconfirmed and unsupported outcomes are not silently counted as wins or losses. CRF trades have their own strategy identity. The optional details display is separate from recording.

Files & history

Live journals, locks and the trade-diary binary snapshot/CSV use MT5 Common Files, scoped to the account/server/symbol; tester storage is separate. The binary ledger is authoritative if CSV export is unavailable. Back up your data before changing builds.

CRF loads and rebuilds M15 history in bounded slabs and can show a rebuilding or unavailable status while it catches up. The optional recent-bar cache verifies overlaps; it does not currently eliminate live history reads or guarantee faster calculation.

!

One coordinator. Shared-file locking coordinates instances using the same local Common Files. It cannot coordinate separate machines. Avoid running the EA on the same account and symbol from multiple machines without external coordination.

08 / WHEN YOU NEED A HANDTROUBLESHOOTING

Start with the status.

The auto row and its tooltip explain the current blocker. These are common places to look before changing settings or intervening manually.

Why is there no order when a signal is visible?+

A signal or forecast is not an order. Check master Auto Trading, mode, the relevant native lane switch, daily timer, MT5 and account permissions, current market state, fresh quote, spread, history, CRF sample/follow-through and reward/risk filters, account type and M15 slot. The displayed blocker provides the next clue.

What do WAIT, unavailable or history rebuilding mean?+

Required readings or bars are not yet available, valid or synchronized. Give MT5 time to load the symbol’s history and inspect the Experts log. CRF chart history rebuilds incrementally; MT5’s available history and max-bars settings still matter. A missing reading is not a neutral signal.

Why does M15 say it is waiting for closing?+

In combined mode, CRF may need the native M15 slot. An existing MT position is closed for takeover only if the specific input is enabled and the verified profit exceeds the cost buffer with all other checks passing; otherwise CRF waits. Pending cancellation and position closure must be confirmed by the broker, then the next M15 close is required.

What does REVIEW mean?+

The EA cannot safely establish state—for example because of an ambiguous request, multiple exposures, incomplete broker history, missing protection, a journal or lock problem. Inspect the broker orders and positions, the details/tooltip and Experts log. Do not assume a request filled or repeatedly submit replacements.

Why did a trailstop pattern not close?+

Check that the position is CRF, trailstop and master Auto Trading are on, activation occurred, and both strict candle comparisons passed. Profit must be positive at the check. Missing data, a failed safety/broker check, or a pattern skipped at zero/loss can prevent a request. Expiry and replacement exits operate separately.

Why is SL shown in a plan but missing on an MT position?+

Stop Loss Placement defaults to false for native MT orders. The plan’s SL can still be used for sizing and validation, but it is not a broker protective stop. The auto row highlights a missing SL. CRF orders require their own broker SL and TP.
